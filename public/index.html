DDog ID

Helping dogs get home.

Registered dogs wear a QR tag on their collar. Scan it with your phone camera to see the dog's details and contact its owner or caretaker.

How it works

  1. 01
    Scan the tagPoint your phone camera at the QR code on the collar.
  2. 02
    Check the profileSee the dog's name, vaccination and sterilization status.
  3. 03
    Contact the ownerCall or WhatsApp in one tap, and share your location if needed.
